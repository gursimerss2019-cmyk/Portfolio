Who I Am

Pratyksh
Sharma

Pratyksh Sharma is the founder and CEO of Infyras. He began his career in web development in 2016 and spent nearly a decade building websites and web applications for clients around the world, growing into a full-stack developer fluent across PHP, WordPress, Laravel, Node.js, Express.js, React, and Bootstrap.
He built his reputation on Upwork, delivering project after project for demanding international clients until he earned Top Rated Plus, the badge reserved for the platform's most consistent, high-performing talent. That track record of clean code, clear communication, and on-time delivery became the blueprint for everything that followed.

In 2025 he founded Infyras to bring that same standard of work to more businesses with a team instead of a single freelancer. In its first year it has grown into a 10-person team of developers, designers, and specialists that has delivered 200+ projects for businesses across the US, Australia, Europe, and the UAE. Pratyksh still works directly with clients, because he believes the best results come from real conversations, clear thinking, and clean execution, never middlemen. When you hire Infyras, you get that founder-led focus on making your website genuinely perform.
